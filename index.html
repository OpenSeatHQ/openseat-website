About

No app required

You have enough apps on your phone. OpenSeat works beautifully in the desktop browser while ticket buyers get a custom checkout page optimized for mobile.

Start selling in minutes

How it works

Four steps from signup to a live event.

The product is small on purpose. You'll be selling tickets in no time.

  1. 1

    Sign up

    Create your passwordless account. You read that right, we don't even make you remember a password.

  2. 2

    Set up Stripe

    OpenSeat is powered by Stripe to accept payments securely (and to pay you out). Create your OpenSeat | Stripe account in just a few mintues. With Stripe you can customize payout schedules, handle refunds, and everything else.

  3. 3

    Publish an event

    We understand selling tickets is a small part of managing your event - so we made it easy. Publish an event in minutes with our 3-step flow and get back to what matter most; curating an unforgettable experience.

  4. 4

    Share the link

    Once you publish an event in OpenSeat we give you a shareable link and QR code. Share it wherever your guests hangout - Instagram, TikTok, your own website. QR codes are perfect for flyers, yard signs, on-site signage, or anything else!

Reporting included. Stripe underneath.

OpenSeat has built-in sales reporting so you can see what sold. Because payments run on Stripe, taxes, refunds, and the rest of the money side stay in a system trusted by millions.
